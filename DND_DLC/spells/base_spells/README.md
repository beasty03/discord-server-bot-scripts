# 🛡️ Base Spells

Foundation spell pack for Wizards. Includes the Shield reaction spell and shop scrolls for Burning Hands and Thunderwave (which are built into the wizard class already but purchasable here as scrolls for future classes).

## Spells

| Spell | Level | Action | Limit | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🛡️ Shield | Lv 1 | Bonus | Once/combat | +5 AC vs next hit |
