# 🩹 Base Healing Recipes

Simple crafted consumables available from the start. No unlock scroll needed — all recipes are known by default.

## Items

| Output | Heal | Sell | Shop |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🩹 Bandage | 1d4 HP | 5 g | 15 g |
| 🫖 Herbal Tea | 2 HP (flat) | 2 g | 5 g |

These items also appear in the daily shop so players who don't craft can still buy them.
