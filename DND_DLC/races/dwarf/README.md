# 🪨 Dwarf

Stout and resilient. Resistant to poison and supernaturally tough underground.

## Stat bonuses

| Stat | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Constitution | +2 |
| Strength | +1 |

## Traits

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Dwarven Resilience | Poison damage halved |
| Stonecunning | +4 on History and Perception checks |
| Darkvision | Passive — flavour only |

## Subraces

### Hill Dwarf
Dwarven Toughness — +1 HP on level-up.

### Mountain Dwarf
Battle-Born — +3 to the first attack roll of each combat.
