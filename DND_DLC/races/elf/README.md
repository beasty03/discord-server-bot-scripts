# 🧝 Elf

Graceful and perceptive. High magical affinity and swift reflexes.

## Stat bonuses

| Stat | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Dexterity | +2 |
| Intelligence | +1 |

## Traits

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Keen Senses | +3 bonus on Perception and Insight skill checks |
| Fey Ancestry | Halved psychic damage (charm resistance) |
| Darkvision | Passive — flavour only |
| Trance | +1 HP on rest actions |

## Subraces

### High Elf
Arcane Affinity — +1 damage on all spell casts (fire bolt, ray of frost, magic missile, etc.).

### Wood Elf
Fleet of Foot — +2 to the first attack roll of each combat.
