# 🧑 Human

Versatile and adaptable. Gets a bonus to every ability and a free feat at character creation.

## Stat bonuses

+1 to all six ability scores (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA).

## Feat (chosen at Lv 1)

| Feat | Effect |
|---|---|
| Alert | +5 to first attack roll of combat |
| Tough | +2 max HP per level (applied at level-up) |
| Sharpshooter | +2 damage on ranged attacks |
| Great Weapon Master | Bonus attack after a critical hit |
