# 🪄 Wizard

Arcane spellcaster with a wide spell list, scaling cantrips, and powerful area spells. Learns additional spells through scrolls.

---

## Stats

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit die | d6 |
| Primary stat | Intelligence |
| Armor | None (low base AC 10) |
| Weapons | Dagger, Quarterstaff only |
| Saving throws | Intelligence, Wisdom |
| Starting gear | Quarterstaff, Spellbook |

---

## Built-in abilities (no scroll needed)

### Cantrips (Lv 1+, unlimited)

| Ability | Damage | Type | Scaling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Fire Bolt | 1d10 | Fire | +1d10 at Lv 5, 11, 17 |
| ❄️ Ray of Frost | 1d8 | Cold | +1d8 at Lv 5, 11, 17 — slows enemy |
| 🧪 Acid Splash | 1d6 | Acid | +1d6 at Lv 5, 11, 17 |

### Spells

| Ability | Lv | Action | Limit | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ✨ Magic Missile | 1 | Action | Unlimited | 3–6 force darts (auto-hit), scales with level |
| 🔥 Burning Hands | 1 | Action | Unlimited | 3–5d6 fire cone, scales with level |
| 🌩️ Thunderwave | 1 | Action | Unlimited | 2–4d8 thunder, scales with level |
| 🪞 Mirror Image | 3 | Bonus | Unlimited | +3 AC vs next hit |
| ⚡ Lightning Bolt | 3 | Action | Unlimited | 8–12d6 lightning line |
| 💀 Blight | 3 | Action | Unlimited | 8–12d8 necrotic |

---

## Scroll-taught spells

Purchased from the shop or found as drops. Permanent once learned.

| Scroll | Lv | Action | Limit | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🛡️ Shield | 1 | Bonus | Once/combat | +5 AC vs next hit |
| ☀️ Scorching Ray | 3 | Action | Unlimited | 3 rays × 2d6 fire |
| 💨 Misty Step | 3 | Bonus | Once/combat | Half damage from next hit |
| 🔥 Fireball | 5 | Action | Unlimited | 8–12d6 fire blast |
| 🚫 Counterspell | 5 | Bonus | Once/combat | Enemy −20 ATK this round |

---

## Subclasses

| Subclass | Perk |
|---|---|
| Evocation | Sculpt Spells — allies never take spell damage |
| Divination | Portent — swap one die roll per combat |
| Abjuration | Arcane Ward — absorbs 3 + level damage before HP is lost |

---

## Level choices

| Level | Choice |
|---|---|
| 2 | Subclass (Evocation / Divination / Abjuration) |
| 1+ | Prepared spells — use `/prepare` to swap your active spell list |
