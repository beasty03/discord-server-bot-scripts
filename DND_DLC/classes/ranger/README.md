# 🏹 Ranger

Agile wilderness hunter with ranged attacks, tracking abilities, and a versatile bonus action kit.

---

## Stats

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit die | d10 |
| Primary stat | Dexterity |
| Armor | Light, Medium |
| Weapons | Simple + Martial |
| Saving throws | Strength, Dexterity |
| Starting gear | Shortbow, Leather Armor |

---

## Abilities

| Ability | Action | Unlocks | Limit | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🎯 Hunter's Mark | Bonus | Lv 1 | Unlimited | +1d6 on every attack while active |
| 💚 Cure Wounds | Action | Lv 1 | Unlimited | Heal Nd8 + WIS (slots scale with level) |
| 🌿 Ensnaring Strike | Bonus | Lv 1 | Unlimited | Prime next ranged hit — restrains enemy (AC & ATK −2) |
| 🌪️ Hail of Thorns | Bonus | Lv 1 | Unlimited | Prime next ranged hit — burst of 1d10 piercing |
| 👻 Vanish | Bonus | Lv 14 | Unlimited | Half damage from next hit taken |
| 🏹 Volley *(Hunter only)* | Action | Lv 11 | Unlimited | Two bonus ranged attacks |
| 🐺 Beast Guard *(Beast Master only)* | Bonus | Lv 7 | Unlimited | Companion intercepts — half damage from next hit |

---

## Subclasses

### Hunter
Colossus Slayer passive (Lv 3+): +1d8 when enemy is below max HP. Unlocks Volley at Lv 11.

### Beast Master
Companion fights alongside you. Beast Guard available at Lv 7. Requires a companion item equipped.

---

## Passive features

| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Favored Enemy | +2 ATK vs chosen enemy type |
| 1 | Natural Explorer | +1 to skill checks |
| 3 | Primeval Awareness | Notified when a favored enemy type is encountered |
| 5 | Extra Attack | Second weapon attack on the Attack action |
