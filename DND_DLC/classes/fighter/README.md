# ⚔️ Fighter

Heavy-armored martial warrior with the highest weapon proficiencies and strong sustained damage output.

---

## Stats

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit die | d10 |
| Primary stat | Strength |
| Armor | All (including heavy) |
| Weapons | Simple + Martial |
| Saving throws | Strength, Constitution |
| Starting gear | Longsword, Shield |

---

## Abilities

| Ability | Action | Unlocks | Limit | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🌬️ Second Wind | Bonus | Lv 1 | Once/combat | Restore 1d10 + level HP |
| ⚡ Action Surge | Action | Lv 2 | Once/combat | Two extra weapon attacks this round |

---

## Subclasses

### Champion
Expanded critical hit range (19–20 at Lv 3, 18–20 at Lv 15). No extra abilities.

### Battle Master
Gains 4–6 Superiority Dice (d8 → d12) and five tactical maneuvers (all require Lv 3+):

| Ability | Action | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 🎯 Precision Attack | Bonus | +1d8 to next attack roll |
| 🦵 Trip Attack | Bonus | +1d8 dmg and enemy AC −2 next round |
| 🔓 Disarming Strike | Bonus | +1d8 dmg and enemy ATK −2 next round |
| 😤 Menacing Attack | Bonus | +1d8 dmg and enemy ATK −2 next round |
| 🔄 Riposte | Bonus | Counter-attack when the enemy misses you |

### Eldritch Knight
Learns arcane spells alongside martial skill (all require Lv 3+):

| Ability | Action | Limit | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Fire Bolt | Action | Unlimited | 1d10 fire (auto-hit cantrip, scales to 4d10 at Lv 17) |
| 🛡️ Shield | Bonus | Once/combat | +5 AC vs the next hit |
| ⚔️ War Magic Strike | Bonus | Unlimited | Bonus weapon attack (Lv 7+) |

---

## Passive features

| Level | Feature | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Extra Attack | Second weapon attack on the Attack action |
| 9 | Indomitable | CON save when reduced to 0 HP — survive at 1 HP on success |
| 11 | Extra Attack (2) | Three weapon attacks |
| 20 | Extra Attack (3) | Four weapon attacks |
